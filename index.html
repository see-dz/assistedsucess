Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does Assisted Success do?

We help clients plan, organize, coordinate, and move forward with events, vacations, projects, goals, family milestones, and small business needs.

Are you a travel agency?

We provide travel planning support such as destination research, itinerary planning, activity ideas, budgeting, and organization. We are not currently acting as a licensed travel agency unless stated otherwise.

Do you offer financial planning?

We offer financial organization, budgeting support, debt-reduction planning, and goal planning. We do not provide investment, tax, insurance, legal, or retirement advice.

Do you provide rentals and decor?

Yes, rentals and décor can be part of event support, including artificial florals, centerpieces, backdrops, bounce houses, tables, chairs, and other event items as available.

Can you help if I have no idea where to start?

Absolutely. Helping clients figure out the next step is one of our main strengths.
